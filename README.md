# EarthScope AI

Team CO2 · NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

Challenge: Be An Earth System Trend Detective!

A research dashboard for six verified NASA Giovanni MERRA-2 country-average
exports for The Gambia (1991–2025): temperature, total precipitation, specific
humidity, two signed wind components, and surface pressure.

The local frontend supports dataset availability, monthly/annual charts, provenance
and methodology. Approved temperature trend and anomaly analysis is preserved;
additional variables provide descriptive values only. Live NASA retrieval,
forecasting and operational sector advice are not implemented.

The six-variable frontend is implemented, reviewed and locally verified.
Production hosting and public deployment remain pending.
